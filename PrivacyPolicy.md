// Privacy Policy for FinDash

This privacy policy outlines how FinDash ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information.

## 1. Data Collection
We collect the following information:
- **Personal Information**: Name, email address, and authentication details when you register.
- **Financial Data**: Transaction history and account balances synchronized through third-party banking APIs (e.g., Plaid).
- **Usage Data**: Information about how you interact with our application.

## 2. Use of Information
Your information is used to:
- Provide and maintain our financial dashboard services.
- Authenticate you and secure your account using Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
- Analyze transactions using Artificial Intelligence to provide personalized budget insights and categorization.
- Generate and export PDF reports at your request.

## 3. Data Storage and Security
- All sensitive financial data and user profiles are stored securely using Firebase Firestore with strict access control rules.
- We utilize industry-standard encryption and security protocols for data at rest and in transit.
- We do not store banking credentials; synchronization is handled via secure tokens provided by banking API providers.

## 4. Third-Party Services
We may share data with:
- **Google Cloud/Firebase**: For database hosting, authentication, and secure storage.
- **Google Gemini AI**: For natural language processing and financial insights. Only anonymized or necessary transaction data is sent for classification.
- **Banking API Providers**: For account synchronization.

## 5. Your Rights
You have the right to:
- Access, update, or delete your personal information at any time.
- Opt-out of specific data processing features (note that this may limit app functionality).

## 6. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact our support team.
